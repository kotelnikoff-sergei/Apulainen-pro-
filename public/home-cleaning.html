Regular Home Cleaning

Regular home maintenance cleaning keeps your home clean and fresh continuously.Maintenance cleaning is carried out at regular intervals, for example, 1, 2x or 4x / month. If needed, you can order additional services according to your needs. We agree together on the time spent on cleaning, so you won't have any surprises after the cleaning.

What does our home cleaning include?

Generally / all spaces

  • Dusting of all surfaces (which the cleaner can reach, height under 180cm)
  • Vacuuming of floors and carpets
  • Damp wiping of floors (if the floor material is suitable for damp wiping)
  • Stain removal from door frames, door handles, and light switches
  • Wiping of mirrors and other glass surfaces (excluding windows)
  • Taking out the trash (if the customer instructs the way / arranges access to the garbage shelter)

Kitchen

  • Cleaning of sink, stove, and cooker hood surface
  • Stain removal from external surfaces of microwave, refrigerator, dishwasher
  • Putting dirty dishes in the dishwasher
  • Wiping of other kitchen equipment (e.g., coffee maker, toaster, blender)
  • Dusting of all surfaces (which the cleaner can reach, height under 180cm)
  • Vacuuming of floors and carpets
  • Damp wiping of floors (if the floor material is suitable for damp wiping)
  • Wiping of mirrors and other glass surfaces (excluding windows)

Bathroom

  • Cleaning and stain removal of bathtub, toilet seat, surfaces, and sink
  • Washing of shower and glass shower wall
  • Dusting of all surfaces (which the cleaner can reach, height under 180cm)
  • Vacuuming of floors and carpets
  • Damp wiping of floors (if the floor material is suitable for damp wiping)
  • Wiping of mirrors and other glass surfaces (excluding windows)

Bedroom

  • Making the bed
  • Changing sheets (if clean sheets are left ready on the bed)
  • Dusting of all surfaces (which the cleaner can reach, height under 180cm)
  • Vacuuming of floors and carpets
  • Damp wiping of floors (if the floor material is suitable for damp wiping)
  • Wiping of mirrors and other glass surfaces (excluding windows)

Tasks not included in the service

To avoid confusion, the following tasks are not included in our service and therefore cannot be ordered at all:

  • Wiping/cleaning of walls and dusting of paintings
  • Wiping/cleaning of ceiling and other surfaces unreachable without aids
  • Removal of extremely difficult stains
  • Cleaning of floor drain
  • Cleaning of odor traps
  • Cleaning of ventilation valves and vents
  • Moving heavy furniture and appliances
  • Post-renovation cleaning
  • Cleaning of furniture

Services that can be ordered separately

  • Ironing (approximately 5-7 shirts) (30 min)
  • Cleaning of refrigerator interior surfaces (30 min)
  • Cleaning of oven interior (30 min)
  • Sauna washing (60 min)
  • Cleaning of trash cabinet interior (15 min)
  • Window washing
  • Move-out cleaning

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to do anything before the cleaner arrives?

Not really. If there are many loose items on the floors or tables, moving them in advance will speed up the cleaning of surfaces, as time won't be spent on arranging items. Otherwise, you can leave everything related to cleaning to us.

Can I be at home during the cleaning?

Of course.

Do you use domestic cleaning products?

Yes. We use a Finnish, biodegradable cleaning product series.

What is the minimum charge for home cleaning?

The minimum charge for a single cleaning session is 4 hours, and for regular cleaning, the minimum charge is 3 hours.

Do you also clean cottages?

Yes, of course, if your cottage is located in our service area. What could be more pleasant than starting your cottage weekend or summer vacation with a pre-cleaned cottage?

What should I do if I need to reschedule or cancel the cleaning?

If you want to cancel or reschedule a cleaning visit, contact us immediately either by phone or email at apulainen.pro@gmail.com. Changes must be notified at least 24 hours before the agreed time.
